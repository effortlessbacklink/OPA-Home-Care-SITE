OPA Home Care, LLC

About Us

OPA Home Care, LLC is a locally owned home care provider based in Roswell, Georgia. We are dedicated to helping individuals live comfortably and safely in their own homes while giving families peace of mind. Our services include personal care, companion care, live-in care, short-term recovery support, and skilled nursing at home.

Our team includes W-2 employed caregivers and licensed nurses who provide professional and dependable support based on each client's needs. We also support veterans through VA CCN and help families understand their long-term care insurance options. Learn more about our services by visiting our official website.

We proudly serve 13 primary counties across North Georgia, including Fulton, Cobb, Cherokee, Forsyth, Gwinnett, and DeKalb. Our goal is to make quality home care accessible and comfortable for every family. Call us at 404-689-4440 to learn more.
